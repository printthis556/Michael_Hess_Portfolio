Charity : Water Game Concept Water 2048 is a browser-based mini game I built for my charity: water project in the Global Career Accelerator. Using vanilla JavaScript, I implemented real-time scoring, persistent high scores (localStorage), Undo/Reset, and fair-feeling random tile spawns. As players reach tile milestones, in-card messages unlock to share clean-water facts. The game is fully responsive (keyboard + swipe), accessible, and uses an SVG sprite system for crisp tile art. I leaned on AI as a coding partner for brainstorming and debugging while owning the architecture, state management, and polish.
L'Oreal Product Aware Routine Builder Chatbot Smart Routine & Product Advisor is a L'Oréal-inspired, product-aware routine builder that combines structured product selection with a chat-style experience. Users start by choosing a category, add items to a “Selected Products” panel, then click Generate Routine to kick off a guided routine-building flow inside the chat interface. The app also includes conversation management with a Clear conversation confirmation modal to prevent accidental resets and keep the UX polished.
NASA Space Explorer App Space Explorer is a NASA-inspired APOD gallery that turns real astronomy data into a smooth, scrollable experience. Tap Explore the Cosmos to fetch live entries, see a 🔄 loading state, then browse cards with titles, dates, and a subtle hover-zoom. Click any card to open a modal with a large image/video, full metadata, and NASA’s explanation. The app also surfaces a rotating Did You Know? space fact and uses a lightweight starfield canvas for atmosphere. It handles video APODs (YouTube/Vimeo) with clean embeds or fallbacks, includes error states, keyboard support, and accessible focus rings, and leans on NASA-style colors and typography.
